# Camera zoom variation

## Summary

Every panel currently shows full figures at the same scale, which reads as a
filmstrip. The paper (§6.2) varies the virtual camera: zoom to the tightest
shot allowed, with periodic establishing shots.

## Requirements

- Zoom in to the tightest shot subject to: never cut a character at the neck
  (cutting at the waist is fine, ankles to be avoided), keep all included
  characters at least partly in frame.
- Establishing shot (fully zoomed out) for the first panel and roughly every
  15 panels.
- Background scales with the zoom; balloons are unaffected by zoom (paper).

## Acceptance Criteria

- Panels vary visibly between waist-up shots and full-figure shots.
- No panel cuts a character at the neck.
- First panel of a thread is an establishing shot.
